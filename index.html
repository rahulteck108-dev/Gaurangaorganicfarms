What Makes Our Products Unique

OUR PROCESS

Free from any kind harmful chemicals, preservatives, additives and anything that is not natural. All our products are made using the age-old techniques which ensure that the nutritional integrity of the food is not compromised. Our processes are as pure as our heart!

01

Ahinsak Hand Milking

We extract A2 milk from our Gir Cows using the ancient Indian practice of “dohan”. According to the Veda’s, “dohan” literally means letting the calf feed to its satisfaction from two “anchals”, while the remaining two are used to obtain milk for our consumption.

02

Made from Curd, not Cream

After boiling the milk, we turn it into rich wholesome curd overnight in an earthen pot. It takes about 30 litres of A2 milk to produce 1 litre of A2 Ghee.

03

Traditionally Wood Churned

The curd is hand churned using a bi-directional teak wood churner also called as bilona to produce delicious makkhan.

04

Slow Cooked on Cow Dung Cakes

This makkhan is then slow cooked in earthen pots over lighted cow dung cakes to produce this golden nectare called Ghee.

05

Packaged in Glass Jars

We purposely package our A2 Ghee in glass jars so that it doesn’t get contaminated as it would in plastic jars.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of this ghee?
Pure hand-churned Ghee made using this process has no shelf life as such. The older it is the better it gets. It’s medicinal properties get enhanced with age. However, for regular consumption like with food its good upto two years.
How should we store this ghee?
Store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. There is no need to refrigerate it. Ensure you always use a clean, dry spoon.
Why is this hand-churned A2 ghee better than home-made ghee?
It is made using the traditional wooden bilona churning method in a bi-directional motion from curd, not cream. This preserves all the medicinal enzymes, making it far superior to cream-based home-made ghee.
